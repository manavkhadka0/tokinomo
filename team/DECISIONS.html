Electronics & Hardware

11 items

Processor — ESP32-S3 (N16R8)

Locked
Options
ESP32-S3 N16R8 · classic ESP32 (WROOM)
Why
8 MB PSRAM + 16 MB onboard flash and better audio handling — enough to run Wi-Fi, decode audio, and drive the motor at once. The classic part is too tight. Onboard flash may remove the need for a separate storage chip.
Owner
Electronics

Presence sensor — mmWave (HLK-LD2410)

Locked
Options
mmWave radar · PIR · ultrasonic
Why
Detects a stationary shopper (measures dwell), immune to light/heat/dust. PIR misses a still person; ultrasonic is narrow and misfires. Trade-off accepted: costs more and talks over UART (not one pin).
Owner
Electronics

Motion — motor for the product gesture

Under review
Was
Metal-gear micro servo (MG90S / MG996R) — single PWM pin, no driver, built-in position feedback.
Now
Mechanical lead — who has built a similar mechanism before — proposes a DC gear motor instead.
Owner
Mechanical + Electronics
Discussion / to resolve A DC gear motor needs an H-bridge driver (e.g. DRV8833 / TB6612FNG) — a servo didn't — and has no built-in position feedback, so motion is timed or limit-switch based rather than angle-commanded. Trade-off: more torque and mechanical flexibility vs. an added driver + control complexity. Decision pending — once locked, update the BOM (add driver), the firmware (drop ESP32Servo, add motor-driver control), and the pin map (PWM + 2 direction pins).

Audio storage — onboard 16 MB flash (LittleFS)

Locked
Options

Why
Onboard 16 MB is enough for the clip(s); no SD-card mechanical/dust risk on a shelf. External SPI flash kept as an optional add-on only if the library grows.

Audio format — WAV (PCM, mono, 16 kHz)

Locked
Options
WAV/PCM · MP3 · OGG/Opus
Why
Zero decode cost on a busy MCU; deterministic, no codec/licensing surprises. Move to MP3 only if flash/bandwidth ever bites. Confirm max length/size.
Owner
Electronics + Backend

Power — 2S 18650 + BMS + AC charging

Locked
Chain
AC adapter → CC/CV charger → BMS → 2S 18650 → buck → 5V/3.3V
Why
Battery so the unit isn't tied to a shelf outlet; mains charging lets it ride through a power cut. BMS protects + balances (non-negotiable for Li-ion left unattended).
Owner
Electronics

Locked

Electronics
